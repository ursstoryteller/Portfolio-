Chapter One

The Man Behind the Frame

— in which our subject confesses to writing scripts at 2 a.m.
Every frame is a sentence. Every cut, a comma. I write films with one hand and dream them with the other.

Purushottam is a Telugu filmmaker working at the intersection of classical screenwriting and emerging visual technology. Five years on set as Assistant and Associate Director have taught him the rhythm of a 100-day shoot; five years at the page have taught him the rhythm of a single line of dialogue.

Today he splits his hours between FadeIn drafts, location scouts, and a second monitor stacked with Midjourney boards — translating script beats into moodboards, posters, and look-development frames before the camera ever rolls.

Based in Hyderabad. Available worldwide. Speaks Telugu, Hindi, English — and the universal grammar of a good close-up.

2022 The Origin Story

Three years under hot lights.

Before the call sheets and the second monitors, there were wooden floorboards, a single spotlight, and the smell of greasepaint. Theatre is where the storytelling instincts were born — the timing, the silence, the long-held look that lasts a beat too long on purpose.

Through college, Purushottam wrote, performed, and helped stage stage plays, mime, musical plays, and street plays — dragging the same character from a black-box auditorium to a busy intersection in a single weekend.

NOW The Industry Years

From the floor to the cut room.

Five years on commercial Telugu film sets — Assistant Director on the floor, Associate Director on the second unit. Call sheets, blocking, continuity, coverage, and the brutal arithmetic of a 100-day shoot.

In the same window, a parallel discipline took shape — feature drafts and series outlines on FadeIn at 2 a.m. And, most recently, AI-driven concept art built around the scripts themselves.
